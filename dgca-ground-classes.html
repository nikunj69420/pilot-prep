Five subjects,one syllabus

Master these and you walk into every exam with confidence, and into the cockpit with real understanding.

Nav

Air Navigation

General and radio navigation, charts, instruments and flight planning, including CP / PNR calculations.

Met

Air Meteorology

The atmosphere, clouds, winds and weather systems, and reading the charts, METARs and TAFs pilots rely on.

Regs

Air Regulations

ICAO annexes, the Aircraft Rules and Civil Aviation Requirements that govern every flight in India.

RTR

Radio Telephony

R/T procedures and phraseology for clear, confident communication with air traffic control.

Tech

Technical General

Aircraft systems, engines, principles of flight and instruments, how the machine actually works.
