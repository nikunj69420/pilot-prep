Becomea pilot

Commercial airliner or private jet, the road to the cockpit takes dedication, training and the right guidance. Here is the route.

Six steps tothe cockpit

From medical checks to your licence, each step shapes a skilled, responsible pilot.

Check eligibility

You need to be 17 or older, with 10+2 in Physics and Maths and good English. From commerce or arts? Clear Maths and Physics through an open school such as NIOS.

Clear your medicals

Class 2 first, anywhere in India with DGCA-appointed doctors. Then Class 1, in Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi, Chennai or Jorhat.

Get your computer number

Your DGCA ID for every licensing exam. Our team checks your documents, files the application and follows it up until it is issued.

DGCA ground classes

Navigation, Regulations, Meteorology, RTR and Technical, then the exams. Live or recorded, in Hindi or English, online or in Mumbai.

Flight training

Build your hours at a verified flight school in India, or in one of seven other countries we work with.

Commercial licence

Earn your CPL, then prepare for airline selection, cadet programmes and type rating with us.

Cl. 2

Medical

A Class 2, then a Class 1 medical, from DGCA-approved examiners.

English

Reading, writing and understanding English is essential to enrol in a CPL course, and to talk to air traffic control every day after it.

Fit to fly,first

Be sure you are medically fit before you spend on a pilot course. It is mandatory, and it comes in two steps.

Class 2 medical

Done anywhere in India with DGCA-appointed doctors. You receive a CA-35 form as fitness certification, your reports go to the DGCA, and the DGCA issues your final medical assessment.

Class 1 medical

The initial Class 1 assessment is available in five cities: Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi, Chennai and Jorhat. You can request it only after the DGCA issues your Class 2 assessment.

Your file number

A permanent, unique number printed on your Class 2 assessment. Every document you send to the DGCA is filed against it for the rest of your career.
