Straightanswers

Still unsure? Ask a counsellor. It is free.

What are the eligibility criteria to join pilot training?

You need to have completed 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics and be at least 17 years old. A valid medical fitness certificate (Class 2, then Class 1) is also required.

Do I need prior flying experience?

No. Our program is designed to train students from the ground up.

How long does pilot training take?

Typically 12 to 18 months, depending on weather, your progress and the licence type (PPL / CPL).

Which licences do you train for?

We prepare you for:

  • Private Pilot Licence (PPL)
  • Commercial Pilot Licence (CPL)
  • Type Ratings (optional)

Is the training aligned with DGCA?

Yes. Our classes follow the official DGCA syllabus and exam pattern, and we only send students to verified flight schools approved by their national aviation authority.

What are the medical requirements?

You first clear a Class 2 medical, then a Class 1 medical for the CPL. Both are conducted by DGCA-approved medical examiners, we guide you through the process.
