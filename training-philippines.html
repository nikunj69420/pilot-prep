Training inPhilippines

With a mix of busy urban airports and relaxed regional airfields, trainees in the Philippines gain experience with varied traffic, radio communications, instrument procedures and different flying environments.

The weather

Tropical clear skies, sudden rain showers and variable winds give aspiring pilots experience in many conditions, beautiful islands and dynamic weather make a comprehensive training ground.

Living inPhilippines

Warm, welcoming and safe, with a vibrant culture and beautiful natural attractions. A strong commitment to education, and the Filipino spirit of “bayanihan,” helping one another succeed.

Why fly inPhilippines

Serene rural airstrips to bustling international airports, dedicated training centres, skilled instructors and tropical weather, with breathtaking islands and warm hospitality.

  • CAAP licence with affordable, quality programs
  • Busy urban airports and relaxed regional airfields
  • Tropical weather that teaches adaptability
  • Dedicated training centres and skilled instructors
  • Warm, welcoming and safe
  • The Filipino spirit of “bayanihan”
